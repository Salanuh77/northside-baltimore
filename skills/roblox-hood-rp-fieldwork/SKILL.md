# Roblox street-roleplay fieldwork

Record the experience, the device, and where you gained control of the character. Mark each observation as played, witnessed, or described by the creator. Treat a loading screen, a permission dialog, a character editor and movement on the street as separate milestones.

Follow one question across games. For first-session navigation, leave spawn on foot and find one useful destination using the cues a newcomer actually sees. Record the landmark, sign, HUD cue or other player action that told you where to go. For a role or purchase, record the entry condition, the input, the visible result and what it cost. Preserve existing characters when exploring save slots.

When observing the player's route, record where street arrows change direction, where a specific NPC first gains an outline, and when each cue clears. Check the handoff on foot even when the tutorial mentions a vehicle. For the November 15 example, see the witnessed Street Life session in the field notes.

Use the comparison to propose a change that fits Baltimore's streets. Name the location, player action and observable success condition. For example, playtest the route from a rowhouse to Juniper Barber with a new save and check whether the shift starts and the first wage appears. Preserve uncertainty about anything you could not reach in a public session.

Read [the field notes](references/field-notes.md) for prior sessions and source links. Record a new session when the question concerns a different build, device or gameplay loop.
