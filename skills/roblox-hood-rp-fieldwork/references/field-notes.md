# Street-roleplay field notes

## Da Hood — Windows Roblox Player, November 15, 2026

**Played.** I joined a public street at night and selected the handset in hotbar slot 2. The avatar raised a physical phone. A handset panel opened along the right edge with the in-game clock and Map, Updates and Customize apps. The sidewalk, health, energy, armor and hotbar stayed visible. Selecting the scoreboard control changed the figures shown beside players. The phone's Map icon was visible, but I did not verify routing to a destination. [Da Hood](https://www.roblox.com/games/2788229376/Da-Hood)

**Baltimore playtest.** Equip the starter phone while standing outside a rowhouse. Check that the avatar shows the handset and the player can still see the street ahead. Open Jobs, select Juniper Barber, close the phone and see whether the street entrance is identifiable on foot. Test this at phone width and on desktop before changing the size or position of the HUD.

## Street Life Remastered — Windows Roblox Player, November 15, 2026

**Played.** After skipping the introduction I got control inside a clothing store. A wall sign priced the cleaning job at $60–70 per puddle. Yellow pins identified the two puddles. I walked into range, activated each prompt and saw the character use a mop. The wallet went from $10,000 to $10,064 to $10,127. The bank stayed at $2,500. The tutorial replaced the cleaning markers with arrows to a scooter stand across the street outside Flipsy's Closet. I crossed the street, rented the free scooter and saw the character mounted on it with driving controls. I did not verify a full scooter trip. [Street Life Remastered](https://www.roblox.com/games/71600459831333/Street-Life-Remastered)

**Baltimore playtest.** Use the existing Community Center desk just inside the spawn entrance. Show the $90 payout while the player is choosing whether to work. Test whether someone facing away from the building can turn toward the desk, pick up the parcel and identify the assigned mailbox. At the mailbox, verify one $90 payment, a cleared compass and no second reward for pressing the prompt again. Leave the guidance dismissible so a resident can head straight to friends.
