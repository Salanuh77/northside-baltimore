# Vehicle artwork

The Harbor GT adapts *Car Concept* by Eric Chadwick for Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, © 2024, built from a CC0 model by Unity Fan. The adaptation bakes transforms, groups geometry by material, omits logos and replaces source textures with Roblox finishes. [Original model and credits](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept) · [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The city sedan, taxi, crossover and patrol SUV adapt the unbranded CC0 vehicle models by Innerscene. The adaptation groups mesh parts, scales them to the game and adds Roblox materials, equipment and a player seat. [Sedan](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/sedan-car-42109739) · [SUV](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/suv-dc972cab) · [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The remaining vehicle geometry is original to Northside Baltimore.
